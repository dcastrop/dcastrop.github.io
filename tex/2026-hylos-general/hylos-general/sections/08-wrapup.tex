  \frametitle{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Program calculation}: laws about divide and conquer
      (hylomorphisms) turn specifications into fast, parallel programs; so
      far, the laws and their side conditions were trusted.
    \item \textbf{Mechanising it}: hylomorphisms in Rocq, axiom-free, every
      step and side condition checked, clean extracted code; each
      definition balances proofs and code.
    \item \textbf{\underline{(Ongoing)} Mechanising low-level calculation}: the goal
      is serialised data, nested parallel loops and a cost model as
      \emph{proved} laws; the first building blocks are proved.
  \end{itemize}
  \begin{center}
    \textbf{Program optimisation as a verified calculation, not an
    untrusted compiler transformation.}
