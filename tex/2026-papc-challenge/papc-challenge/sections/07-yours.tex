\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{We Welcome Your Challenges}
  This is our \emph{first} challenge, not our only one. A good challenge
  \begin{itemize}
    \item is something \textbf{you} care about: a game, a puzzle, an
      algorithm from a module, a theorem from a lecture;
    \item says what \textbf{correct} means, in a sentence;
    \item has a \textbf{first level} you can reach in a few weeks;
    \item can be done \textbf{by hand}.
  \end{itemize}
  \textbf{Ideas}: a compiler from arithmetic expressions to a stack
  machine, proved correct; a sorting algorithm, proved to sort; a type
  checker for a tiny language.
  \par\medskip
  Tell us on Discord or on a Thursday, and we will help you cut it into
  levels.
\end{frame}
